# Trash

Items you delete go to Trash first, so you can change your mind.

## Moving Items to Trash

- Right-click → **Move to Trash**.
- Select items and press **Delete** or **Backspace**.
- Tick several items and press **Move to Trash** in the selection bar.

Only your own items can be moved to Trash; items in folders shared with you
can't. Files that are locked or still processing can't be trashed until
they're ready.

## In the Trash View

| Action                 | What it does                                          |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| **Restore**            | Takes the item out of Trash so you can use it again.  |
| **Delete Permanently** | Removes the item for good, after you confirm.         |

You can open trashed folders to look inside. Tick several items to
**Restore** or **Delete** them together from the selection bar. When Trash
is empty you'll see "Trash is Empty."

## Skipping Trash

**Delete Permanently** (and **Delete** in the selection bar) also appears
outside Trash. It deletes items right away — there's no undo.
