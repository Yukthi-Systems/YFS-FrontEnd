# Sharing Files & Folders

Open the sharing dialog from an item's right-click menu (**Share**), the
share button in List view, or the Details panel. The dialog has two tabs:
**People** and **Public links**.

## People

Share a **folder** with people in your organization.

1. Search for someone by email (type at least two characters).
2. Choose permissions under "applied to people you add."
3. Pick the person from the results.

Only people in your organization who have signed in to YFS at least once
can be found. Each person's permissions can be changed later, or their
access removed (and undone before saving).

## Permissions

| Permission   | Allows                         |
| ------------ | ------------------------------ |
| **Preview**  | Open and view contents.        |
| **Download** | Save a copy locally.           |
| **Create**   | Add new files here.            |
| **Edit**     | Rename and change files.       |
| **Delete**   | Remove files.                  |

New shares start with Preview and Download.

## Public Links

Public links let anyone with the address open a file or folder — no YFS
account needed. They're also the only way to share a **single file**.

- **Add link** creates a new link. You can customize its address
  (3–36 characters) before saving; after that it's fixed.
- **Permissions** — same options as for people.
- **Password** — optional; can be set, changed, or removed later.
- **OTP emails / phone numbers** — optional; visitors must verify with a
  one-time code sent to one of these.
- **Expires** — optional; up to 3 months ahead. The link stops working at
  the end of that day.

Copy a link once it has been saved. Manage all your links later from
**Shares → Shared by link**.

## Saving Changes

Changes in the dialog are drafts until you press **Save changes**. Labels
like **New**, **Edited**, and **Removing** show what will change, and
**Discard** throws your edits away.

## Things to Know

- You can't re-share a folder someone else shared with you.
- Items that are locked or still processing can't be shared until they're ready.
- If sharing is turned off for your organization, sharing options are hidden.
