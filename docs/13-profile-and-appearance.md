# Profile & Appearance

Click your avatar at the bottom of the sidebar to open the account menu.

## Account Menu

| Option           | What it does                                          |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| **Profile**      | Opens your profile and storage details.               |
| **Theme**        | Light, Dark, or System (follows your device).         |
| **Accent color** | Changes the app's highlight colour, or resets it.     |
| **Sign out**     | Ends your session.                                    |

## Profile

- **Avatar colour** — pick a colour for your avatar. Your name comes from
  your Yukthi SSO account and can't be changed here.
- **Storage** — used space, total quota, percentage, and file count. The
  bar turns red at 90% or more.
- **Account** — organization, domain, phone, two-factor status, whether
  file versioning and sharing are enabled, and your user ID.

## Recalculating Storage

If your storage figure looks wrong, press **Recalculate** in Profile, or
the refresh icon next to Storage in the sidebar. This re-scans every file
you own, so it may take a moment, and asks you to confirm first.

## Synced Preferences

Your theme, accent color, view mode, sort order, folder colours and icons,
and sidebar layout are saved to your account and apply on every device.
